Football

  1. Which team came back from a 28–3 deficit to win Super Bowl LI?

    2. New England Patriots

    B. New England Patriots. They beat the Falcons 34–28 in February 2017 in the first Super Bowl to go to overtime.

    2. Kansas City Chiefs
    3. Los Angeles Rams

    D. Tampa Bay Buccaneers. They beat the Chiefs in Super Bowl LV in February 2021.

    4. Green Bay Packers

    A. New York Giants. The Giants won Super Bowl XLII 17–14 in February 2008.
